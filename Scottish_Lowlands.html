About the Tour

  • The ride starts on dd.mm.yyyy so we will be staying in Edinburgh on the night before to allow an early start to the ride the next morning.
  • This tour is over a 3-day period finishing in Edinburgh on dd.mm.yyyy, riding roads through the Scottish Lowlands with 3 overnight stays.
  • The tour is primarily a road tour but occasionally uses well paved cycle paths that are suitable for road bikes.
  • Transport of luggage between accommodation stops is included, so riders can ride free of heavy luggage.
  • Accommodation is limited so early reservation is recommended.

Estimated Cost & Pricing

  • For three nights accommodation and bike/luggage transport, the estimated cost is around £300 per person.†
  • This estimate is based on a maximum group size of 10 riders and assumes shared twin-room accommodation.

    • † All our tours are designed to make no profit and any surplus funds are distibuted back to each rider.

What's Included in the Tour

  • 3 nights' accommodation with breakfast.
  • Transportation to/from the start/end point for both bikes and luggage from a loading point in Belper - making it easier for you to arrange your own personal travel.
  • Transportation of luggage between accommodation stops - ride with just your essentials and not with the burden of excess weight.
  • A support vehicle to assist with any issues that may arise during the tour.†

    • † Each day, the support vehicle will meet riders at the ride half-way point.

What's Not Covered

  • Personal travel to/from the tour start/end point.†
  • Meals.

    • † We’ll offer guidance on travel options and group travel arrangements.

Route Overview?

  • The routes are designed to take you through the most scenic parts of the Scottish Lowlands. The first day crosses the Forth road bridge, skirting the Firth of Forth estuary before turning west to Stirling, the second day goes to the Trossachs, Loch Lomond and Glasgow and the third takes you back to Edinburgh via the Clyde and Firth Canal.
    All routes include options to switch to a shorter alternative with less elevation.

  • A support vehicle is available to transport your luggage between each overnight stop and will be on-call if mechanical assistance or rider pick-up is required.
