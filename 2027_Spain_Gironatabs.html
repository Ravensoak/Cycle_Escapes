About the Tour

  • This is a road tour based in Girona with rides organised over a n-day period, on roads through the Spanish countryside and coastal areas.
  • Our overnight self-catering accommodation is based in Girona with secure bike storage.
  • Cycling in this area is challenging with lots of climbing, so riders should be well-prepared. Riders will be encouraged to pace and not race so that everyone can enjoy the experience.
  • Accommodation is limited so early reservation is recommended.

Estimated Cost & Pricing

  • For seven nights accommodation and transport of rider, bikes and luggage to and from Girona Airport, the estimated cost is around £350 per person.†
  • This estimate is based on a maximum group size of 7 riders and assumes shared twin-room accommodation.

    • † All our tours are designed to make no profit and any surplus funds are distibuted back to each rider.

What's Included in the Tour

  • 7 nights' self catering accommodation in Girona.
  • Transportation of riders, bikes and luggage to and from Girona Airport to our accommodation in Girona.

What's Not Covered

  • Personal travel and transport of bikes and luggage to/from the departure airport and flights to/from Girona Airport.†
  • Cost of bike hire (if required).††
  • Meals.
  • Local / Country Taxes.
  • Insurance.

    • † We’ll offer guidance on travel options and group travel arrangements.
    †† We’ll offer guidance on bike hire options.

Route Overview?

  • The routes are designed to take you through the most scenic parts of the Spanish countryside and coastal areas. Routes may include options to switch to a shorter alternative with less elevation, or extensions if more miles are desired.
  • Each day, we’ll propose specific routes, but these may be adjusted to reflect weather conditions or riders’ overall fatigue levels.
  • Some cafés do not take cards, so ensure you bring cash (Euros) with you on the ride.
